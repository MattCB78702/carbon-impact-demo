# Carbon Impact — Demo talk track (New Belgium Brewing)

**Live demo:** https://mattcb78702.github.io/carbon-impact-demo/
**Audience:** New Belgium (Sayali's deal; materials already with their VP of Marketing)
**The one-liner:** *"This is what your sustainability lead uses every day — and the Share views are what they hand everyone else."*

**The argument in one breath:** Buying credits isn't the hard part. Defending the purchase — to legal, to finance, to marketing, to every employee at a bar — is. Carbon Impact is one trusted portfolio record, translated for every audience, with the evidence attached.

---

## 1. Portfolio (open here)

- Point at the KPI band: **15,597 t retired to date** — "that blue chip means *registry record*: this is your real, public ACR and Verra history. We didn't make it up; anyone can verify it. That's the standard the whole workspace holds."
- Scroll to the project cards: "Every project shows *why it's yours* — the ✓ badges are your own criteria from our catalogue: value-chain states, CCP approval, no renewables, removals balanced against price."
- The Montana grazing card is the flagship story: **barley country**.

## 2. Footprint

- "Credits only make sense in context. Your reported footprint is ≈286,000 t; 87% is supply chain."
- The coverage table is the honesty moment: brand program fully covered · ≈27% of Scope 1+2 · ≈3.5% of total. "Nobody can accuse you of greenwashing with this on the table — and your 2030 company-wide commitment means this program grows an order of magnitude. That's why the discipline matters now."

## 3. Evidence Vault (the defensibility engine)

- Retirement records: click a **blue-edged row** open — real record, real purpose text ("CY24 and CY25 Fat Tire Ale and Mountain Time Lager Emissions"). Then a **Proposed** row — orange chip, serial assigned at execution. "Real and proposed never blur."
- Claims table at the bottom: point at the **"Not approved"** row ("We are a carbon-neutral company"). "The workspace knows what you *can't* say. That's what keeps marketing and legal out of each other's inboxes."

## 4. Share (the centerpiece — spend the most time here)

- Landing: "Five audiences, one source of truth. Nothing written twice, nothing drifts."
- **Marketing kit** → the Don't say / Say instead pairs. This is the VP of Marketing's page.
- **Legal & claims pack** → AB 1305 disclosure readiness, claim-to-evidence map.
- **Finance & audit pack** → allocation, bands, "every dollar maps to a registry serial."
- **Employee page** → "the FAQ your bartender can repeat without overclaiming."
- **Exec one-pager** → the peer chart: **BrewDog retired 450,000 t — from Darkwoods, a project that's in your catalogue.** Then the sustainability-lead narration: why these credits.

## 5. Plan & Decide (close)

- "This cycle started from your criteria, not from inventory. Next cycle starts here too — criteria in, recommended portfolio out, everything downstream regenerates automatically."
- Close: "The catalogue you have is the merchandise. This is the machine that makes it defensible, explainable, and renewable — and it comes with working with CarbonBetter."

## Q&A quick answers

- **"Is this live software?"** — "It's a working preview populated with your real registry history and our real catalogue; the product ships with your first purchase."
- **"Where do prices come from?"** — "Bands here are indicative; firm quotes are in your confidential catalogue" (never quote exact prices from the public URL).
- **"Where does the footprint data come from?"** — "Public reporting aggregations; the live workspace runs on your GHG inventory."
- **"What about our current HFC supplier?"** — "Continuity is in Alternatives Considered — we kept the option open; this mix diversifies the story your brands can tell."
